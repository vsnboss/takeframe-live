03 / MATCH

BRING THE MATCH.
TAKEFRAME BUILDS
THE TRUTH.

Point TAKEFRAME at what you have — a match URL, a team sheet PDF, a spreadsheet, an official page — and it assembles the match: clubs, kits, squads, kick-off, venue, referee. Every value is either verified, flagged for review, or honestly missing. A missing fact stays visible until a source supplies it. Nothing is invented to fill the gap.
