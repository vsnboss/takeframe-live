From unstructured sources
to a match ready for air.

Sports production information arrives as links, PDFs, messages, photographs, spreadsheets, screenshots and imperfect feeds. TAKEFRAME creates order before the first graphic goes live.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

From chaos to
broadcast-ready in 6 steps.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Add links, documents, photographs, spreadsheets, raw text and available data feeds.

  2. 02

    Extract

    Identify teams, players, shirt numbers, positions, line-ups and match metadata.

  3. 03

    Match

    Connect players, clubs, competitions, assets and statistics to the correct records.

  4. 04

    Reconcile

    Compare sources and expose disagreements instead of hiding them.

  5. 05

    Validate

    Keep the operator in control of uncertain information and final on-air truth.

  6. 06

    Prepare

    Supply the scorebug, line-ups, player graphics, formations and live events.

One preparation workflow. One verified match. Every graphic ready.
03 / ONE SOURCE OF TRUTH

Correct it once.
Use it everywhere.

TAKEFRAME works from a shared match model. When an operator corrects a name, changes a shirt number or approves a formation, the updated information becomes available to every relevant graphic.

Match identity

Competition, venue, date, teams, colours, crests and presentation data.

Squads and line-ups

Players, shirt numbers, positions, starters, substitutes and officials.

Media assets

Portraits, club crests, competition branding and sponsor assets.

Live match state

Score, clock, goals, cards, substitutions and key match events.

Operator review

Warnings, missing information, conflicts and confidence indicators.

Package-ready data

One validated workspace serving every supported scene and graphic.

06 / SOFTWARE-DEFINED PRODUCTION

Built for the production models
replacing legacy hardware.

TAKEFRAME is developed around portable operator devices, browser technology and networked production—not dedicated graphics appliances, permanent graphics rooms or fill-and-key infrastructure.

01

Browser-based control

A modern interface designed for production laptops and future tablet workflows.

02

Networked graphics delivery and modern software production environments come first.

03

04

Remote operation
