Player image readiness

The wrong kit never has to go on air.

  • The operator selects the actual match kit.
  • Source portraits are reviewed against that kit.
  • Adapted portraits require approval.
  • Missing or unsafe portraits cannot reach Preview or Program.
  • Graphics can switch to approved photo-free variants.
