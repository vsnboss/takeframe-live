Privacy

How enquiry data is handled.

TAKEFRAME is operated by VSN. The public demo form does not upload match documents or media. When no secure form backend is configured, it opens a pre-filled email to office@vsn.hr.

Information requested

Name, company, work email, country, organisation type, estimated annual match volume, current production software, typical source types and an optional message.

Purpose

VSN uses these details only to respond to the enquiry, assess a possible pilot and prepare a relevant product demonstration.

Sharing

Enquiry information is not sold to advertisers. It may be processed by service providers needed to operate email and hosting, subject to their contractual and legal obligations.

Retention and requests

Information is retained only as long as needed for the enquiry, commercial discussion and applicable legal requirements. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact office@vsn.hr.
