04QUESTIONS

Before you buy.

The things production teams ask us most often about how TAKEFRAME is licensed.

Do all paid plans have the same features?

Yes. Annual, Monthly and Match Pass all contain the complete TAKEFRAME football product. Nothing important is held back for a higher tier.

Is there a perpetual licence?

No. TAKEFRAME is subscription and match-pass only. You pay for the right to use the current product for a defined period or production.

What counts as one Match Pass?

One canonical TAKEFRAME match, activated for a 72-hour production window.

When does the 72-hour window start?

When the operator activates the pass for a match inside TAKEFRAME — not when you buy it.

Can I install TAKEFRAME on a backup computer?

Yes. A standard paid licence allows two registered Windows production computers.

Can both computers run different matches at once?

No. A standard licence covers one simultaneous clean Program production. For more, add concurrent-production entitlements.

Do I need internet during a match?

TAKEFRAME is designed for local-first licensed broadcast operation. A temporary outage does not interrupt a production that is already on air.

What happens when my subscription ends?

Your local TAKEFRAME production data remains yours and stays on your machine. Clean licensed Program output resumes when you renew.

Can a league run multiple matches simultaneously?

Yes, with multi-production licensing. Talk to VSN and we will size it to the number of concurrent productions you run.

Does the evaluation support NDI® and OMT?

Yes. The evaluation is the full product, with a TAKEFRAME EVALUATION watermark on output.
